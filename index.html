How this works

—

  • Starting July 1, loan servicers send notices telling SAVE borrowers to enroll in a legal repayment plan within 90 days (as the Education Department, ED, describes it publicly). Borrowers who don’t switch are automatically enrolled into the Standard Repayment Plan or the new Tiered Standard Plan.
  • Days left counts from the date you picked to your notice date + 90 days, when you say you have a notice. The words carry the meaning, not just the color: days left, Past deadline, or Waiting for notice.
  • Earliest first-wave deadline: Sep 29 2026 (ED court filing / Student Loan Planner; Business Insider Sep 10 2026). Later notice waves have later deadlines — your notice still controls.
  • If you do nothing, ED says you go into Standard or Tiered Standard. That’s ED’s wording — not a plan recommendation from this page.
  • Compare options at StudentAid.gov/idr and read studentaid.gov court-actions. This page does not enroll you.
  • We only use what you type in — no FSA login, and we don’t pull anything from your servicer. If a date is missing, we won’t guess a deadline.
  • This page explains the calendar only — not plan advice, not forgiveness advice, not a servicer tool, and not a decision about your payment. Your notice controls your deadline. We never invent your payment amount or a “best” plan.
